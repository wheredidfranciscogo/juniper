A quiet place for your plants.

Juniper tells you when to water your plants, and then gets out of the way. One tap to mark it done. It remembers, so you don't have to.

The whole app

It's one loop, and it works.

Most plant apps are a catalogue with a reminder bolted on. Juniper is a reminder, with just enough app around it to be worth opening.

  1. 01A reminder arrives on the day a plant actually needs water — not a blanket daily nudge.
  2. 02You tap it and land on that plant, not on a home screen you have to navigate.
  3. 03You tap Water. It ripples, it settles, it's done.
  4. 04It reschedules itself for that plant's own rhythm. You don't think about it again.
Considered

Built to be calm.

It never scolds you

No streaks to break, no red badges, no guilt about the fern. A plant that's thirsty is just thirsty.

Paper, not neon

Warm paper and clay, drawn botanical line-art, and a herbarium pressing for every plant. Not another saturated-green grid.

A slow record

Every watering, feeding, repotting and note kept in a quiet log — so you can see how long you've been tending something.

Free to use

The core is a utility, and utilities shouldn't ask for a subscription before they've been useful.

Privacy

Your garden stays on your phone.

There is no account to make and no server to sign in to, because there is no server. Your plants, your photos and your log live in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere for it to go.
