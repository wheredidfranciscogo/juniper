Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

Juniper does not collect your data. Not in a careful, well-governed way — it simply doesn't collect it. This page explains what that means in practice.

What we collect

Nothing.

Juniper has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, and no backend server of any kind. We have no database with your name in it, because we have no database.

Where your information lives

Everything you enter — your plants, their watering schedules, your photos, your notes and your activity log — is stored in Juniper's own private storage area on your device, and is only readable by the app.

  • It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • We cannot see it, retrieve it, or recover it for you.
  • If your device is backed up by iCloud or Google, that backup is between you and Apple or Google under their terms — we are not a party to it.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data. There is no copy anywhere else.

Notifications

Watering reminders are local notifications: your device schedules and shows them itself. They are not push notifications, there is no push token, and no server is ever told which plants you own or when you water them. If you decline notification permission, the rest of the app works normally.

Photos and the camera

If you add a photo to a plant, Juniper asks your device for permission to use the camera or photo library. The image is copied into the app's own storage on your device. Photos are never uploaded and are not readable by us.

The one time the app talks to the internet

Juniper offers an optional plant-species search when you add a plant. If — and only if — you use it, the app sends the words you typed to a third-party plant database (Perenual) to look up care information, and receives results back.

  • No personal information, device identifier, or anything about your existing plants is included in that request.
  • Your search is subject to Perenual's own privacy practices while it is with them.
  • Skip the search and add plants manually, and Juniper makes no network requests at all.

Children

Juniper is not directed at children, and since it collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Privacy law gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete the personal information a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase. Your own copy is on your device, under your control: you can edit or delete any plant in the app, or remove everything at once by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If Juniper ever gains a feature that transmits data — cloud backup or sync being the realistic candidates — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the feature will be optional and off by default.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@juniper.app
