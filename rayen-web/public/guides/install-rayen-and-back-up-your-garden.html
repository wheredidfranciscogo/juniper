Install Rayen on your phone and back up your garden

By Francisco Arrieta · 13 November 2026 · 2 min read

Rayen is a web app, so there is nothing to download. Two small things make it behave like an app and keep your garden safe: adding it to your home screen, and saving a backup now and then.

Add Rayen to your home screen

On iPhone

  1. Open rayen-garden.vercel.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Rayen then opens full screen from its own icon, with no browser bars.

On Android

In Chrome, open the menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. I have built and tested Rayen on an iPhone, so if the wording differs on your phone, the idea is the same.

Why it is worth doing

A website’s saved data can be cleared by the browser. Safari on iPhone, for example, may delete what a site has stored after about a week without a visit. Web apps added to the home screen are exempt from that rule. Since Rayen keeps your garden in the browser’s storage, installing it is the simplest protection there is against your plants quietly disappearing.

Save a backup

  1. Tap Rayen, the name at the top left of the deck. It opens the Backup sheet.
  2. Tap Save a backup.
  3. On an iPhone the share sheet opens: choose Save to Files and pick a folder, such as iCloud Drive. On a computer the file downloads.

The file is named like rayen-garden-2026-11-13.json. It holds your plants, your watering history and your photographs, so keep it somewhere you would keep anything personal. The Backup sheet also shows when you last saved one.

Restore your garden

On a new phone, or after your browser data was cleared, open Rayen and tap the name at the top (or Restore a backup on the empty screen). Choose Restore from a file and pick your backup. Rayen tells you what it is about to replace and asks before it does, then your garden is back.

Restoring replaces whatever garden is on that device, so use it on a new or empty one. A backup written by an earlier version of the app still opens.

Starting over

Start over in the same sheet clears the garden and its photographs, after asking you to confirm. Save a backup first if there is any chance you want it back.
