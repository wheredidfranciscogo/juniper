A free plant watering tracker

A quiet record of the plants in your care.

Rayen keeps track of when you last watered each plant and which one needs you next. It runs in your browser: no account, no download, nothing sent anywhere.

Open Rayen How it works

Free · Open source · No ads

How it works

  1. Add the plants you have.A name, the species if you know it, how many days between waterings, and how much light it gets. Add a photo if you like.
  2. The card in front is the one that needs you.Rayen keeps the deck in order of thirst. The number in the corner is how many days until that plant wants water, and its colour drifts toward dry as the day gets closer.
  3. Turn it over to water it.Tap the card, press Water, and Rayen writes down the date. The plant goes to the back of the deck until it is thirsty again.
  4. Or look at everything at once.The grid shows every plant as a tile, so one glance tells you which are fine and which are thirsty.

Small on purpose

Rayen does one job. These are the choices that keep it that way.

What it gives you

  • No account.Nothing to sign up for and no password to lose.
  • Nothing to install.It opens in your browser. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like any other app.
  • Works offline.Once it has opened, it keeps working without a signal.
  • Your data stays with you.Your garden lives on your own device and is never sent to a server.

What it leaves out

  • Reminders.There are no notifications. The deck is the reminder: open it and the plant that needs you is in front. If you want a nudge, a calendar does that better.
  • Plant identification.It records the plants you tell it about. It does not guess from photos.
  • Syncing between devices.A garden lives on one device. A backup file is how it moves to another.

Your garden stays yours

Rayen has no server holding your plants, so there is nothing to leak, sell or shut down. The other side of that is worth knowing: if you clear your browser's site data, or move to a new phone, your garden goes with it. A backup is one tap, it is a file you keep wherever you like, and you can restore it on any device. Here is how.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no sign-in.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Will it remind me to water?

No, and on purpose. Notifications on a phone need a server that knows your device, and Rayen has none. The bet is that you will open it because looking at it is pleasant, and the thirstiest plant will be in front.

Where is my garden stored?

In your browser, on the device you are using. Photos are stored there too. Nothing is uploaded.

What happens if I lose my phone or clear my browser data?

Your garden is gone unless you saved a backup. Tap the Rayen name at the top of the deck to save one, and keep the file somewhere safe.

Does it work on Android and on a computer?

It is a standard web app and runs in any modern browser. It was built and tested on an iPhone, so that is where it is best, and your feedback on the rest is welcome.
