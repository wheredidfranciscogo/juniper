Rayen

A quiet record of the plants in your care.

Rayen is a small, free web app for keeping track of your plants and when you last watered them. Nothing else.

Your garden is a deck of cards. The card in front is the plant that needs you most. Turn one over for its record, water it from its back, and it steps aside until it is thirsty again.

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to lose.
  • No download. It runs in your browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app.
  • No data collected. Your garden is stored on your own device and never sent anywhere.
  • Works without a signal. Once opened, it keeps working offline.
  • Yours to keep. Save your garden to a file whenever you like, and restore it anywhere.
  • Free and open source. The whole thing is on GitHub under the MIT licence.
