Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Rayen collects nothing about you. Your garden is stored on your own device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The app

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.
  • Your plants, their watering history and any photographs you add are kept in your browser's own storage, on the device you use. We cannot see them.
  • A backup is a file you choose to save. It goes where you put it and nowhere else.
  • Clearing your browser's site data, or removing the app from your home screen, deletes your garden. Only a backup you saved can bring it back.

What a visit does reveal

Opening any website involves a little unavoidable plumbing, and Rayen is no exception:

  • The app's files are served by Vercel, which, like any web host, may briefly log technical details of a request such as an IP address and browser type.
  • The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google for them.

Neither receives anything about your garden.

Sign in with Google

The Rayen app never asks you to sign in with Google and never accesses a Google account.

Separately, the person who maintains Rayen uses a private research tool, registered with Google under the Rayen name, to decide what to write about. It is not offered to the public. For completeness, here is what it does:

  • It signs in with the maintainer's own Google account and reads that account's own Google Search Console reports and Google Ads keyword planning data.
  • It is used only for that research: seeing which searches reach this site and how often people search for a topic.
  • The data stays on the maintainer's computer. It is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.
  • Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome as an issue on the project's GitHub page.
